## Brand Tokens

- Lilac ground `#F7F5FC` · white cards · stage lilac `#EFE9FB`

- Mochi lilac `#DCCCF5`. The Z logo stays as its navy tile.

## Typography

- Bricolage Grotesque 800 for display, Figtree for body, both via `next/font` in `app/layout.js`.

## Design Direction

Proof first: the 18-dot mystery-shop scoreboard is the signature, Mochi and Orb (`components/Mascot.jsx`) carry the personality. Warm, honest, built on real numbers.

## Component Guidance

- Use fewer transparent panels and stronger solid surfaces.
- Keep blue rare and decisive: CTAs, active journey states, key proof.
- Make proof sections feel like product evidence.
- Avoid decorative blur and excessive gradient text.
- Use clear contrast between copy and background.
