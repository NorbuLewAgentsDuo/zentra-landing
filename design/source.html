The problem

The leak isn't the ads. It's what happens after.

A buyer enquires. You answer. They say “let me think about it” and go quiet. Property decisions take months — but the follow-up stops after a week, because it depends on somebody remembering.

01
Follow-up runs on memory
Two chases, then the lead quietly falls off the list. Nobody decides to abandon it — it just happens.
02
Enquiries land while you're busy
You're driving, at a viewing, or asleep. The reply comes hours later, if at all.
03
Your CRM stores. It doesn't move.
Leads sit in a list waiting for you to act. The list gets longer; the pipeline doesn't.
